Alan Jahde is a co-founder of Anderson & Jahde, P.C. His practice is focused on a combination of international income and estate tax planning, asset protection planning, private placement life insurance planning and defined benefit pension planning. Mr. Jahde also assists international clients with offshore trusts and international tax planning structures. Alan earned his J.D. from the University of Georgia and his LL.M. in Taxation from the University of Denver. His work has been published in the _Journal of Asset Protection_ and the _Journal of Financial Planning_, among others. Mr. Jahde was named one of Denver’s Top Rated Lawyers of 2014 by [_Legal Leaders_](https://www.law.com/legal-leaders) in Trusts and Estates and General Practice.
