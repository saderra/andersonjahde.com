    answer:

  - question: "What's the difference between a will and a revocable living trust?"
    answer:
      - "A will directs how your assets are distributed but generally must go through probate. A revocable living trust can transfer assets outside of probate and offers more privacy and control while you're alive, including if you become incapacitated."
  - question: "How often should I update my estate plan?"
