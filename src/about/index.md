Steve Anderson and Alan Jahde opened Anderson & Jahde in Denver, Colorado in 1995 as a boutique tax law firm. From the start the practice centered on one thing: representing taxpayers who found themselves across the table from the Internal Revenue Service and state taxing authorities.

Since then the firm has grown to include attorneys who spent decades inside the IRS Office of Chief Counsel and the Department of Justice Tax Division, along with partners who built their own tax controversy practices before joining us. In 2024 the Merriam Law Firm, a Denver tax controversy practice founded in the 1980s, merged into Anderson & Jahde.

Anderson & Jahde has been named among the Best Law Firms in America by Best Lawyers in America and awarded its highest honor of Top Tier Law Firm in Tax. Lawyers in the firm have received some of the highest individual professional recognitions in America, including Best Lawyers' Denver Tax Lawyer of the Year and the Colorado Bar Association's James Bye Lifetime Achievement Award in Tax. With successes in landmark tax cases all the way up to the United States Supreme Court, our list of satisfied clients is long.

The firm has also represented hundreds of CPAs, tax return preparers, and accountants in civil and criminal investigations by the IRS; professional malpractice cases; complaints filed at Colorado's Board of Accountancy, the AICPA, the IRS's Office of Professional Responsibility, and the Securities and Exchange Commission; and in many other facets of their professional lives.

Estate and trust planning, asset protection planning, trust and probate administration, and probate litigation are also central practice areas at Anderson & Jahde. Our work helping clients plan for the future, and then being there for them and their loved ones when the future arrives, is our privilege.
